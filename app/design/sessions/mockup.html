Sessions · open, closed, delete (plans/ko-sessions.md, phase 1; 2026-11-12, proposal)

A session is persistent: closing it (today's "kill") keeps its name, layout and runtimes on disk; it shows under Closed in the sidebar and as "Open › name" in the palette; Delete is a confirmed action on a closed session only. A client that finds no open session offers the closed ones instead of making a new one, unless there are none.
