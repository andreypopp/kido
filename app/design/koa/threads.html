Threads in koa's page

docs/design-koa.md, "Threads" · the widget, the marks, the filter · approved 2026-11-18

A thread is a named workstream of one session; the agent labels its own text with [thread: name] lines, which the transcript turns into marks. The page shows the threads in a capsule at the top left (the view bar's family), lists them with their status and summary in a panel under it, and filters the transcript to one thread on a click. Nothing here is live.

1 · The widget

A capsule at the top left of the pane, pinned outside the scroll, in the root agent's view only (a subagent's view has no threads: no widget, no marks, no filter; the view bar is a subagent's, so the two never share the corner): as many threads as fit, newest first, each as "# name" in its colour with "·" between, then "+N" muted for the ones that do not fit, and "▾"; the capsule is at most the pane's width minus 16pt, a name is never cut, and only the first one, which always shows, ellipsizes when it alone does not fit (user, 2026-11-18: show more while the width allows, collapse only on overflow). Threads have no status and no dot anywhere (user, 2026-11-18): "# name" in the thread's colour is the lead, in the widget as in the transcript. It does not show for a session with no thread. A click opens the panel under it: one row per thread, newest first: "# name" in the colour at 600, the summary muted and ellipsized, the age of the last section at the right. A click on a row sets the filter; Escape or a click outside closes the panel.

2 · The marks in the transcript

Every reply carries a pill at its right, on its first line, its time ending on the same vertical as every other time: "# name HH:MM" in the thread's colour, the time alone in the faint colour when the reply has no thread. A reply about two threads has a pill on each section, each with the time. The pill is the control: a click sets the filter to its thread (a hand, hover underlines the name at 50%, pressed 100%). Nothing else is marked: a user message keeps its plain time (its threads are the ones its reply names; chips on it were tried and removed, 2026-11-20), and the run rows between the messages (the tool calls, the messages to and from agents and the jobs of a turn, folded into one row) show no thread and no colour edge, although each keeps its thread for the filter.

3 · A filter active

The capsule becomes the thread: "# name" in its colour and an ×; the panel marks the row. The transcript shows only that thread's items, without pills (they would all say the same), and, until it is labelled, an implicit user message of any thread, with nothing above it; the label arrives with the reply and the message then stays or goes. The composer's foot gets a first cell "# name" before the model, and the placeholder keeps the page's verb rule with the thread added: "Steer @ko in #name…" while the agent works (the case here), "Message @ko in #name…" when it is idle; the prompt goes with the thread and koa writes the marker. The × in the capsule, the "All threads" row of the panel, or a click on the current row clears the filter.
