koa — agent messages and job notices

How koa's transcript shows what passes between agents and what background jobs report, beside the user, assistant and run items of mockup.html. Outgoing (koa's agent calls spawn_agent or message_agent) and incoming (ko-pi's custom messages: a message, a steer, a notice, a report) are agent messages: the user message's block with the header "kind to|from @name relation" and the time at the right, and one line of the text, ellipsized: two lines at most (user, 2026-11-10); a click expands to the whole text and back. An async job notice (async_bash's end notice, a streamed batch) is one terminal line: the job's name, its end and the time, gray for success and red for a failure; a click opens its output and the log path. The plain agent tools (list_agents, read_report, stop_agent) stay steps in a run. The texts are the ones ko-pi writes today (share/pi/ko-pi.ts); the fields are the planner's section 9.

1 · Outgoing: a spawn and the messages

A spawn_agent or message_agent call is a card, not a step: it splits the run it sits in (here between the read/bash run and the assistant's text). The header is one phrase in the prose font: the kind, then "to" or "from", then @name in 600, then the relation: "message to @sidebar-right", "steer to @html-refine", "task to @correctness-review" (a spawn); at the right only the time (user, 2026-11-10). No delivery status (delivered, queued, sent, spawned) and no session, window or model line; a refusal takes the kind's place, in red: "refused to @name". A card is two lines at most: the header and the first line of the text, ellipsized (section 5).

2 · A failed spawn and a refused message

A spawn that ko refuses or that fails to start is the same block with "refused" in red in the kind's place and the reason as the body, in ko-pi's words. A refused message_agent keeps its text and shows "refused" the same way.

3 · Incoming: from the parent, a child, a peer; a steer

"kind from @name relation", the relation one word in the faint tone after the name (subagent, peer, parent; none for ko-pi's own notices), the time alone at the right; the kinds: message, steer ("is redirecting this work"), report, ended (a child's end: how, and whether it had reported, in the text; "not reported" in red in the kind's place; the screen tail behind the ellipsis), notice (ko-pi's own). A report is a child's work product: the same block with the kind report (shown expanded here); ko-pi's "full report: read_report(…)" trailer is not shown (user, 2026-11-10).

4 · Job notices: ended, failed, streamed

One terminal line: a chevron, the job's name, the end in ko-pi's words ("exit 0", "exit 1", "signal SIGKILL", "failed: …", "timed out 600 s"), the time at the right. Gray when it succeeded; the name and the end in red when it failed. A click opens the output under a hairline guide, as a step opens: the tail in a well at most 200pt, then the log path and "… N lines omitted" faint. A stream batch has the kind stream after the name and no end; consecutive batches of one job join one line, the batches separated by their times when open. A service job is marked service. The second one here is open.

5 · Folded and unfolded

A message is two lines at most when collapsed (user, 2026-11-10): the header, and the first line of its text cut with an ellipsis; the ellipsis is the sign that there is more. A click anywhere on the block expands it to the whole text; a second click collapses it. A text that fits one line shows no ellipsis; the block still toggles, so a text that wraps in a narrow pane stays one line until clicked. A job notice is one line, closed by default, and opens on a click. Left: collapsed and closed; right: the same, open.

6 · The plain agent tools stay steps

list_agents, read_report, stop_agent, list_async_bash and stop_async_bash are ordinary tool steps in a run, by name with their first argument, as blocks.html draws them; nothing passes between agents in them.
