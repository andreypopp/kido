koa — Markdown inside a thinking step

An expanded thinking step renders its text through the reply's Markdown renderer. The rule: the thinking tone stays. Everything in the block is --muted, bold is weight alone, code and quotes go a shade quieter rather than louder, headings are body size, and the 260pt scroller is the one scroll: a code block never scrolls on its own vertically. The reply's .prose rules for paragraphs, lists, quotes, links, rules and tables apply as they are; the values below are what changes.

1 · Paragraphs, a list, inline code, bold

Paragraph gap .65em as the reply; list indent 1.4em, markers in --faint; inline code .92em in the terminal font on the 5% well (the reply's is the 8% well), in the muted colour; bold at 600 in the muted colour, not the foreground. The first child has no top margin, the last no bottom margin, so the step's 2pt/4pt detail margins hold.

2 · A code block, a heading, a quote, a table

A fenced block: .88em terminal font, the 5% well, a 1pt --hair edge, 7pt radius, 8pt 10pt padding, no language label, no height cap of its own (horizontal scroll only); the step's 260pt scroller scrolls the whole. Headings at body size, 600, margin .8em 0 .3em, muted. A blockquote: the 2pt bar at --hair2, its text one shade quieter, --faint. A table at .95em with --hair rules and faint headers. A link is muted with a hairline underline, not the accent.

3 · Long: the scroller

The block is capped at 260pt and scrolls; 12pt right padding keeps the text clear of the scrollbar. Nothing inside has a cap of its own.
