koa — the questions in a reply get controls

When a reply is final, an extension (pi-infer-questions first) can annotate it: each question's range, its kind, and the options of a choice. koa renders the reply as before and puts the controls after each question, in the prose, where the text is: a choice shows each option in place, then Other; a choice with one option shows the option, then Yes / Other; a yes/no question or a proposal shows Yes / No / Other; an open question shows Answer. A click sends nothing: it puts the question as a quote and the answer into the composer, and a second click on the same question replaces its answer (the TUI's rule, kept). The question's own text is not styled. Click the controls here: the composer follows.

1 · A reply with every kind of question

An option is a span of the question's own words, in the accent colour with a 1pt underline at 40% (a link has 35%; an option's is a shade stronger and the text is a button, not a link). The labels after a question (Other; Yes, No, Other; Yes, Other; Answer) are chips 8pt after the question's end: .88em, the accent with a 35% accent edge, 5pt radius, 0 6pt padding, 4pt apart; a question inside a list item or a table gets them where its text ends. Hover: a 12% accent fill (shown on "wait for CI" and on the first "Yes"). Nothing is pre-selected.

2 · After two clicks: the composer holds the quotes and the answers

The clicked control is selected: the 18% accent fill (the user message's tint), the text in the foreground, no underline and no edge. "wait for CI" and the proposal's "Yes" were clicked: the composer has one block per question, the question as a quote, a blank line, and the answer; the cursor at the end. A click on "push now" now would replace "wait for CI" in the first block and move the selection; "Other" replaces the answer with nothing and leaves the cursor under the quote; "Answer" (an open question) appends a quote with an empty answer. The user edits and sends as usual.

3 · Narrow

At 60 columns the chips wrap with the text as words do; an option that wraps keeps its fill across the line break. Nothing changes in size.
