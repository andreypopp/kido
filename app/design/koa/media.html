koa — images, attachments and Quick Look

The visible parts of plans/koa.md, section 8, for units 12 to 14: images in the transcript (a read of an image, a user message with images, an image that is gone), attachment chips above the composer (an image, a file, a path, an upload, a refusal, the drop state, a refused folder, the queue, the sent message), and Quick Look (⇧⌘ over a step's file, the browser lightbox). Every value follows the approved language of mockup.html and blocks.html; the thumbnails are drawn placeholders. Nothing here is a product change yet.

Click a run or step line to fold it; hold ⇧⌘ over a step's path to see the Quick Look affordance.

12a · A read of an image, the step open

The step line reads read path with the image's type and pixel size as the detail in place of "N lines". Open, the detail shows pi's note in the faint tone and the image as a thumbnail: at most 240pt tall, its own aspect, a hairline, radius 6pt; the pixel size appears at its corner on hover. A click (or Space/Enter when focused) previews it; in a browser, the lightbox. Two images from one tool (codemode's image()) sit side by side and wrap.

12b · A user message with images and files

The text first; then the thumbnails in a row, at most 160pt tall, wrapping; then the path lines that a file attachment became, in the terminal font and the muted tone, as pi stores them. The time stays at the right of the first line.

12c · An image that is gone

A 404 or 410 from /images/{id} (koa's directory was cleaned, or a resumed session without the file): a dashed 120×80pt box in the thumbnail's place with "Image unavailable", no click. Beside it a thumbnail that is still there, so the two read as the same row.

13a · Chips above the field

Each attachment is a 28pt chip in the dock, above the field: a 20pt thumbnail or a document glyph, the name, then the size in the faint terminal font, and × at the end. A path chip (a local session's drop) shows the name and its directory muted, so the user sees that pi gets the original. An upload shows a spinner in the glyph's place and the bytes so far. A refused chip has a red-tinted edge and the reason in red in place of the size. A click on a chip's body previews it; × removes it.

13b · Drop hover

While a drag with files is over the pane, the field's border is dashed at 45% fg and the placeholder reads "Drop to attach"; nothing else changes. The whole page is the target.

13c · A folder in a drop

Folders are not handled for now, in any session: the files of the drop become chips, and the folder is refused with one line in the chip row, "Folders are not attached", which goes after 4 s or at the next drop.

13d · The queue with attachments

A queued prompt that carries attachments shows a paper-clip glyph and the count after its text, in the faint terminal font; take back restores the chips with the texts.

13e · Sent: the user message with its images and files

After Send the chips go and the message shows the thumbnails and the path lines of 12b; the composer is empty again, and koa works.

14a · ⇧⌘ over a step's file

While ⇧ and ⌘ are both held, the path of a read, edit or write step with a file gets the pointer and a link's underline in the muted tone; ⇧⌘-click opens the file in Quick Look (the file as it is now on pi's host), the same gesture as Shift-Command-click on a terminal's images. A plain click folds. Other steps show nothing under ⇧⌘. The second step here is drawn in that state.

14b · The browser lightbox

Without Kido (a browser), a click on an image opens a fixed overlay on the dock's glass with the image at its natural size, at most the viewport less 24pt each side, a caption with the name and pixel size below; a click anywhere or Escape closes it, and Escape does nothing else while it is open.
