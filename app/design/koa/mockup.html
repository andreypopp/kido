koa — reference for the webview harness

One pane, one page. The page uses only the --ko-* properties the app sets from the pane's theme and font; the :root block at the top is the fallback for a plain browser (kido-light / kido-dark). Every tone is a color-mix of --ko-fg, --ko-bg and the ANSI palette. Prose is the system font at the terminal's size; tool lines, code, the composer footer and the status bar are the terminal font.

Click a run line or a step line to fold or unfold it.

1 · Idle, empty session

No header. The pane's own chrome already names the agent; the page starts with its wordmark, the composer focused, and the status bar.

2 · Streaming assistant answer with markdown

The user message is a left-barred block in the accent colour, its time of sending in the terminal font at the right of its first line (14:32 today, Nov 7 14:32 on an earlier day). The answer renders as it streams, with a blinking caret in --ko-cursor at the end of the text.

3 · A run of tool calls and thinking, collapsed

A run is every consecutive thinking block and tool call between two texts. Collapsed it is one terminal-font line: a spinner or a check, a summary of what is in it, the elapsed time. While running, the summary names the current step.

4 · The run expanded to one line per step

Each step is one line: a mark for its kind, the command or path, the result on the right. Marks: $ bash, ✎ edit, ◇ read, ∴ thinking. Results: a green check with the time, a red exit N, a muted aborted.

5 · One step expanded to its details

Bash: the command, then output in a well, cut to 260pt and scrollable. Edit: a diff with tinted added and removed lines. Thinking: the full text in muted prose.

6 · A failed tool and an aborted run

A failed step keeps the red result inline and the run's own line turns red with the count. An abort (Escape while working) leaves the last step marked aborted and the run's line says so.

7 · The composer

Empty and focused; multiline up to six lines, then scrolling; while the agent works. One 26pt rounded square (8pt radius, the palette's Send control) at the bottom right of the field, no border and no text: send (accent, arrow right) while there is text, dimmed to the 18% hairline tone while the field is empty, and stop (fg square with a stop glyph; red on hover) while koa works. The key hints are faint terminal-font text at the left, shown only while the field is focused or koa works.

8 · The status bar

One terminal-font line, 24pt: state · model · thinking level · context use (bar and percent) · cwd and branch at the right. In the narrow pane the numbers behind the context bar and the thinking label go first.

9 · The narrow pane (60 columns)

Same page at 60 terminal cells. The transcript loses its centring margin, tables scroll inside their block, the run lines ellipsize their summary before the time, the status bar drops its optional segments.

Open questions and the data each part needs are in NOTES.md beside this file.
