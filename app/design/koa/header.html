A topic is a named workstream of one session; the agent labels its own text with [topic: name] lines, which the transcript turns into marks. The page shows the topics in a capsule at the top left (the view bar's family), lists them with their status and summary in a panel under it, and filters the transcript to one topic on a click. Nothing here is live.

1 · The header row and the chip

Approved (user, 2026-11-21). Every message, user or agent, starts with a header row that holds only its time at the right (the time's design as today) and, when the message has a topic, the topic's chip at the left: "# name" in the topic's colour on a tinted pill (the colour at 10%; on the solid user block the page background at 22% with the ink), the user's choice of 2026-11-21 among the Chip picker's treatments. The reply's pill is gone. A reply about two topics has the chip of its first section in the header, and each later section starts with a row of its own chip, without a time. A user message whose topic is known (the classifier's label, a filter, a typed marker) has the chip too; the two multi-topic user messages here have none. Runs, steps, notices and job lines are unchanged: no header, no chip.

2 · A filter active: the chip in the composer

Two things (user, 2026-11-21): the composer's topic and the transcript's filter. The composer's topic is its chip at the top left of the field, above the text, with an × that drops it; it is set by a click on a message's chip, by the "+ topic" chip (section 2b), and by the widget's filter. The filter is only the widget's: a name in the capsule, a panel row, a block row; the capsule's × (line mode) and "All topics" (panel, block mode) clear it. A filter sets the composer's topic too, as here; the chip's × then drops the composer's topic and leaves the filter; clearing the filter drops the composer's topic when it is the same topic (user, 2026-11-22), and leaves a different one. The placeholder is the plain "Steer @ko…" / "Message @ko…" and the foot's topic cell is gone. The messages of the filtered transcript show the time alone in their header.

2b · Starting a topic explicitly

The composer alone creates a topic (user, 2026-11-21): its header row always exists and holds either the composer's topic as a chip with an ×, or the same dashed "+ topic" chip as a message's header shows on hover (here it is always visible: it is the route). A click on it opens the naming chip in place (left: the placeholder "topic name", the caret in it); typing a name (a Zulip topic, with spaces and any text: there is no #name syntax, it would collide with Markdown headings) and Enter sets the composer's topic (right): an existing topic's name takes that topic's colour, a new one the next colour; Escape or the × goes back to "+ topic". There is no /topic command: "+ topic" is the only way to name the composer's topic (user, 2026-11-21). The chip is the composer's topic: the message goes to #name, a new topic or an existing one; the transcript is not filtered, on send either (user, 2026-11-21); the classifier is not asked; the chip stays after the send.

From an existing message (user, 2026-11-21): hovering a message without a topic's header row shows a dashed "+ topic" chip (user, 2026-11-22: a message with a topic shows none; its topic changes by dragging a topic row from the widget onto it; a multi-section reply with any with a topic section shows none); a click opens it as the naming chip in that row; Enter creates the topic and moves the message's turn into it (the MVP's move), Escape or the × drops it. Left: the hover on the user message "Also look at the Linux flake…"; right: naming it.

2e · Unread counts in the widget

User, 2026-11-23: a topic row shows its unread count in the right-most place, where the age is, as a red circle with the count; the age is replaced, not kept beside it. What counts is the divider's rule: assistant messages with visible content, once at their append, not streaming growth; per topic, the items of that topic. Seen in any view clears: an item seen in All topics or in its topic's view is seen in both (one unread state per item). The badge: 16px high, min 16px wide, 0/5px padding, 999px radius, the terminal's red (`--ko-color-1`) with white 600 10px tabular text, "99+" over 99; on hover of the row the × takes the slot as before. The active topic's row shows no badge (its items are the shown transcript, and the divider says what is new); "All topics" shows none. An archived row keeps its badge at full strength (it is the one thing a dimmed row must still say). Line mode: the capsule's names carry the count as a smaller red circle after the name (14px, 9px text), the panel's rows as block mode. Here: 3, 12, 1 and 140 unread; the third pane has the sidebar filter active, so its row has no badge.

2c · Archiving a topic

User, 2026-11-21: every topic row in the widget (the panel, and block mode's rows) has an × at its right, shown on hover in the age's slot; a click archives the topic. An archived topic stays in the widget, dimmed to 45%, listed after the live topics (newest first within each group), and out of the line-mode capsule's names and "+N"; its hover control is ↺, which restores it. The classifier is not shown archived topics and cannot label into one; only ↺ restores: a message sent to an archived topic (its chip in the composer, a filter on it, a turn moved into it) leaves it archived. Filtering to, renaming and merging an archived topic work as for a live one. Left: the panel with the "release-0-32" row hovered and two archived topics; right: block mode.

3 · The panel: drag to merge, drag onto a reply, rename

From the classifier MVP, reviewed 2026-11-21 (NOTES, "The classifier MVP, reviewed"). A press on a row and 5px of travel start a drag: the row dims to 50%, the pointer is the grabbing hand, no ghost. The target carries the dragged topic's colour: a 2px outline at 50% and a fill at 6%, inset on a panel row (over the hover fill), 3px outside a transcript message. A drop on a row merges the dragged topic into it; a drop on a message moves its whole turn (user message and replies). A double-click on a row renames it in place: the field takes the row's width ("#" kept in the colour, the summary and the age hidden), the name selected; Enter or a click outside commits, Escape cancels. A single click filters after 250ms.
