koa — the composer's foot at narrow widths

The foot drops its cells by its own width (a container query on the field, not the window): first the detail of each cell, then the model and the cwd, then the context, until the state alone is left. What goes stays reachable: /model and /thinking open the pickers, /session has the context numbers and the model, and the cwd is the pane's title in the sidebar. Each pane below is the pane width that gives the foot that width (pane = foot + 74pt: the composer's 24pt and the field's 12pt on each side, the 1pt borders).

1 · The four steps, idle

Full (foot 540pt and up): state · model · level · context bar, numbers, percent · cwd. Step 1 (under 540): the thinking level, the cwd's leading directories and the context numbers go; the bar and the percent stay, "kido" stays. Step 2 (under 340): the model and the cwd go; the state at the left, the context at the right. Step 3 (under 200): the state alone, its reserved width released so a long state text ellipsizes instead of overflowing. The cwd is capped at 40% of the foot in every step and ellipsizes from the end.

2 · The other state texts at each step

The state cell's text changes with the mode, and the cell keeps its reserved width through steps 1 and 2 so nothing else moves: "run", "run, quiet" (! mode), "completing…" with the spinner (Tab waiting for bash), "compacting 4s" with the breathing dot, "working 12m 34s" (the widest). At step 3 the widest ones fit in a 200pt foot; under that they ellipsize.

3 · A long cwd

A deep directory: the cwd cell is capped at 40% of the foot and ellipsizes at its end, so the name's start shows and the model and context never move; under step 1 only the last component is there, under step 2 none.
