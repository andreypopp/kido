koa · the tool block and the thinking block, every state

Each row is one state, kido-light left and kido-dark right, drawn with the shipped share/koa/koa-page.css (branch webview) and the markup koa-page.js emits: a run line (.run > .line) over its steps (.steps > .step), each step a line with chevron, the action name, its label and the outcome, and a .detail that opens on click. Captions name the state and the facts the page reads. Nothing here is a proposal; it is what koa draws today.
