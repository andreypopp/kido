Connection color

The window title, the session names in the sidebar, the focused-pane ring (two or more tiled panes) and the sidebar focus edge use the connection's color. The title names the connection as typed, such as "devbox". Text must reach 4.5:1 on the canvas and the ring 3:1 on the pane paper. Local is the dimmed blue preset. Preset colors are fixed per theme (a light and a dark value); only custom colors are adjusted until they reach the contrast.
