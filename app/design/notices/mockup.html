Notices · errors over the pane area

Approved on 2026-11-07 and implemented (docs/design-app.md, "Notices"). Every failure that has no UI of its own becomes a card over the bottom of the pane area: a transient one goes after 5 s or a click; the config error stays until × or a load that succeeds. The card never takes the keyboard. NOTES.md has the classification of every caller, the geometry and the rules. Live buttons use the real timers (5 s, hover pauses).
