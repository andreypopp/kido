Design proposal, no live search. The find bar is a glass capsule in the hover toolbar's family: 32pt tall, 12pt radius, 26pt icon buttons, 0.5pt separators, anchored at the pane's top-right where the toolbar sits. It is persistent while open; the hover toolbar keeps its trigger zone and, while find is open, appears below the capsule at the same right edge. Behaviour is unchanged: Cmd-F opens or focuses; Return / Shift-Return and Cmd-G / Shift-Cmd-G navigate; Escape or × closes and clears highlights; copy mode keeps its COPY label and “(loaded)” counts.
