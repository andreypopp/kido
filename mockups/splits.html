Kido.app — split panes

Every variant uses the real geometry: tmux places panes on a cell grid with a one-cell divider between them, and the app places each pane's grid. A variant that needs a wider gap than one cell adds points per divider and reports fewer columns or rows to tmux. The readout under the window gives that cost. Click a pane to focus it. The panes have no titles and no buttons.
